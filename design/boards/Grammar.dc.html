THE LAW OF LIGHT
Every glyph is a measurement with a formula behind it. A glyph with no formula row does not ship. Verification state is the product's most important feature, so its states get the sharpest visual distinctions on the board.

NOT STARTED
Absent from the sky entirely. The feature exists only as a dashed row in the Flight Plan. The sky records work, never intentions.

IN PROGRESS
The moon appears the moment work starts: a dashed ember silhouette with a dim core. Visibly unfinished at any zoom.
First commit or branch touching the feature's paths, or an agent mission opening against it; timestamped as STARTED
FAILING
A broken red arc with the failure count at the gap. Still unverified; failure is drama the sky reports, never hides.
Latest recorded check for the feature exited non-zero; ticks count consecutive failures; log tail one click away
VERIFIED
The distinct cue: the category fill goes under a 32% verification-green wash, inside a bold halo with a timestamp tick. A change in kind from in-progress, not a brightness tweak. The flip animates once, in the same instant its evidence line prints.
A recorded check {command, exit 0, sha, timestamp} at current HEAD. Never settable by hand; REVOKE exists and returns the moon to dashed
EVIDENCE STALE
Proof rots. The halo decays to dotted amber when commits land after the last green run. The body and its green wash stay; the claim weakens visibly at the rim.
git diff --name-only lastRunSHA..HEAD intersects the feature's paths; label reads VERIFIED @ sha · N behind
PRODUCTS ARE PLANETS
FORMING
The planet appears, as a dashed accretion disc, when work starts on its first feature. Products with zero started features do not exist in the sky.
First STARTED event of any feature belonging to the product; orbit index = product formation order, stable forever
IN PROGRESS
Solid body once features exist, wearing no ring of any kind; the verified fraction prints in its label. Ring geometry is reserved entirely for full verification, so its arrival is a flip from nothing, not a thickening.
Label fraction = features VERIFIED at HEAD / features STARTED; recomputed on every evidence event
PRODUCT VERIFIED
The whole-product cue, a change in kind three ways at once: a soft verification shade blooms around the entire system, the bold band ring appears where no ring existed, and the product's own orbit line lights. Reserved for the moment everything under it is proven working.
ALL features VERIFIED at HEAD, AND the product-level walk (its own end-to-end check) recorded exit 0 at HEAD
CORONA RETRACTED
Honesty runs backwards too. New work started, or evidence went stale, after full verification: the shade fades and the band withdraws to a dotted ring until the product is whole again.
Any feature leaves VERIFIED (new STARTED event, staleness, failure, REVOKE) while the product held PRODUCT VERIFIED

THREE LAWS
1. Nothing appears until work starts, and nothing turns solid without a recorded exit 0.
2. Nothing animates without a named git or process event. A still sky means no work is happening.
3. Verification can be taken back. Stale, failing, and revoked states are drawn as loudly as success.
