EARNED LIGHT
Products are planets. Features are moons. Nothing appears until work on it starts, and nothing turns solid until a recorded check proves it. When every feature of a product is verified, and the product's own walk passes, its corona lights.
01 · APPEAR ON WORK

02 · PROOF DRAWS LIGHT
