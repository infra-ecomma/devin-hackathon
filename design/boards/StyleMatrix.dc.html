THE INVARIANT
The Law of Light does not bend to a skin. Dashed still means started and unproven, a halo still requires a recorded exit 0, amber still means evidence went stale, the corona band still requires everything verified plus the product walk. Verification green is a role with a per-skin value, and nothing else in a skin may wear it, so a green-colored category could never be mistaken for a verified state.
Type follows style: Orbitron with JetBrains Mono in Futuristic, Cinzel with Courier Prime in Rustic, Inter for body in both. A skin swap changes tokens and typefaces, never geometry, so spatial memory survives the toggle.
